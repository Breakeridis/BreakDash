BreakDash recommended driving maps

https://tiles.openfreemap.org/styles/bright
https://tiles.openfreemap.org/styles/dark

BreakDash modifications: stronger road edges; 20% wider roads/casings from
zoom 14; 20% larger street labels; fuel/car-parking POIs at zoom 15, names
at 16; flat, subdued buildings; near-black night palette with amber major
roads and light local roads. Night retains Dark's base, with Bright's road,
bridge, tunnel and shield stack. No endorsement by upstream authors.
