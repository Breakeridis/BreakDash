# BreakDash v1.0.13

A native Bluetooth manager and diagnostic foundation for the K2401 head unit.

- New **Settings → Bluetooth → Open Bluetooth** screen with Devices, Phone & Music and Diagnostics sections.
- Separate Classic and BLE scans, each limited to 12 seconds, with paired-device states, device names, signal strength when available and a maximum of 32 listed devices. Device addresses are visible locally when a device is selected.
- Android pairing for Classic devices and a Bluetooth-settings shortcut for forgetting devices. Paired and connected remain distinct states.
- **Test BLE connection** connects to the selected scanner and discovers available service definitions within a shared 25-second deadline, then releases the connection. It records stages, timings and numeric failures without sending OBD commands or reading vehicle data.
- Factory Phone, Bluetooth Music and MAXOBD shortcuts retain the existing working apps while their interfaces are investigated for the full integrated experience.
- **Inspect factory interfaces** collects bounded package/service metadata and generated interface signatures from accepted read-only factory APKs. It invokes no unknown vendor controls.
- Feedback now includes a passive Bluetooth/permission snapshot and sanitized saved test/inspection results. Device names/addresses, contacts, advertisements and characteristic values are excluded. Scanning and connections stop when the Bluetooth screen is covered, closed or paused; no background scanning or reconnection is added.

To investigate the KW906: temporarily turn off smartphone Bluetooth and close other OBD apps on the head unit. Open **Settings → Bluetooth → Open Bluetooth**, grant the requested permissions, and enable Location on Android 10. Choose **Scan BLE**, select the scanner and tap **Test BLE connection**. Then open **Diagnostics**, tap **Inspect factory interfaces**, and use **Send feedback** to preview and upload the evidence. A successful BLE test confirms connection/service discovery, not live OBD communication.

Install **BreakDash-v1.0.13-armeabi-v7a.apk** on the head unit, or **BreakDash-v1.0.13-arm64-v8a.apk** on compatible 64-bit devices. Use **Updates → Check again**, or install over the existing signed release to retain preferences.

GitHub Actions runs the Bluetooth state-machine/privacy regressions, existing map/JVM/reporting checks, Android lint/builds, native architecture checks and signing verification. No local builds or tests run. The head-unit Bluetooth path, pairing, failure codes, factory interface visibility and phone/music coexistence require physical-device confirmation. See the [Bluetooth design and acceptance checks](https://github.com/Breakeridis/BreakDash/blob/v1.0.13/docs/bluetooth-diagnostics.md).
